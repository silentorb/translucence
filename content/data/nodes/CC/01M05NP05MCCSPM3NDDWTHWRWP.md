Every worldview must address the question of whether [[01M05NANSCJE2KKVN9KTWPDQ0E]] and [[01M05NAX56RNNSCJ7FF4K68AN1]] exist, and if so, the following question of how they can coexist?

That is a [[01KZXWFJGHQXTGH942SMX0VG56]].

Every human heart has a basic understanding of [[01M05NANSCJE2KKVN9KTWPDQ0E]] and [[01M05NAX56RNNSCJ7FF4K68AN1]] a belief that they both exist.

# In every heart

Some worldviews claim that [[01M05NANSCJE2KKVN9KTWPDQ0E]] and/or [Evil](./01M05NAX56RNNSCJ7FF4K68AN1.md) do not exist.

Such claims are surface claims which conflict with the root beliefs held by all.

> "For God knows that when you eat of it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and evil."
>
> Genesis 3:5

It is impossible for a human to comprehensively live his life as though he does not believe in either [[01M05NANSCJE2KKVN9KTWPDQ0E]] or [Evil](./01M05NAX56RNNSCJ7FF4K68AN1.md).

Anyone who denies the existence of either [[01M05NANSCJE2KKVN9KTWPDQ0E]] or [Evil](./01M05NAX56RNNSCJ7FF4K68AN1.md) will disprove his claimed worldview through his conduct.
