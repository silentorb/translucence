An analogy is when two different things are compared in relation to properties they share.

An analogy requires two things which:

1. Share one or more properties.
2. Have properties that differ between them.

The latter point is important because if two things share all properties and have no differences, then they are not actually two different things and are the same thing.

There is no value in having an analogy between A and A.
