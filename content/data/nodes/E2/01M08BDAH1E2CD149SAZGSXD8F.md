An analogy fallacy occurs when someone derives conclusions from an [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] which the [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] does not support.

The most common case is when someone presents an analogy between two things, then makes a case dependent on the premise that those things share certain properties they don't actually share.
