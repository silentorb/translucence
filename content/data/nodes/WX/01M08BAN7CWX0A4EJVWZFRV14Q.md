Sometimes people present an analogy between [[01M05NAGC9RYEXEN2A0SPTR1MW]] and a puppeteer.

While there is nothing inherently wrong with the analogy because there are parallels between [[01M05NAGC9RYEXEN2A0SPTR1MW]] and a puppeteer, that analogy is sometimes used as an [[01M08BDAH1E2CD149SAZGSXD8F]].

The [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] does have parallels in that in one sense [[01M05NAGC9RYEXEN2A0SPTR1MW]] directs human actions, and a puppeteer directs puppet actions.

However, [[01M05NAGC9RYEXEN2A0SPTR1MW]] and a puppeteer only have limited similarities.  Otherwise [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] would not be possible.

Two major differences between [[01M05NAGC9RYEXEN2A0SPTR1MW]] and a puppeteer are:

1. A puppeteer is human
2. A puppeteer's puppets are not human

One area where the [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] breaks down is it is recursively bleeds into itself.

The [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] compares two dimensions, God -> Humans and Puppeteer -> Puppets:

| God   | Puppeteer |
| :---- | :-------- |
| Human | Puppet    |

However, those are not discrete, independent dimensions.  The puppeteer is a human, so the two sides of the [[01M08BC3JPZ8JVHC555VHVX9FS]] actually form a continuum:

> God -> Puppeteer (Human) -> Puppet
