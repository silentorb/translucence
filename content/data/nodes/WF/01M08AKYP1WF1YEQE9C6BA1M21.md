# Free will

> Then the Lord God formed the man of dust from the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living creature.
>
> Genesis 2:7

Man cannot create life, much less life out of dust.

Even if man could create life, man does not control the entire reality surrounding that created life.
